CipherHunt'26

Check a certificate issued for this event.

  1. Find the certificate ID printed on the certificate.
  2. Enter the ID, the name and the certificate type (Participant or Organizing Committee) as they appear.
  3. Compare the result with the certificate you are checking.

You can leave out the CH26- at the start.

Capital letters and extra spaces do not matter.

About the event

CipherHunt'26 was a capture the flag (CTF) cybersecurity competition held on 14 November 2026 at the Faculty of Computing & Technology, University of Kelaniya. It was organized by the UOK ISACA Student Group.

A certificate is confirmed only when its ID, name and type match what the issuing organization generated.
